How It Works

The simulator allows you to test various traffic scenarios by adjusting the following parameters:

  • Vehicles: Number of vehicles in each lane
  • Pedestrians: Number of pedestrians waiting to cross
  • Jaywalkers: Pedestrians crossing illegally (slight priority penalty)
  • Emergency Vehicles: Presence of emergency vehicles (highest priority)
  • Starvation Control: Gives bonus points to lanes waiting longer
  • Previous Green Light: Indicates which lane had the green light before
